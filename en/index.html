Discover EIXORN

Find and use your records again.

EIXORN is an independent software studio focused on records from everyday life and creative work. We make them easier to find and use when they are scattered or damaged.

Bring scattered records together to plan what’s next.

Schedules live in calendars, while thoughts and decisions end up in notes. We bring them together so people can see what needs doing today and get ready for the next task.

Keep the memories of a visit with the artwork.

After a gallery visit, people can return to the works and the memories visitors shared. Artists and organizers can keep artwork materials and visitor records together by exhibition and use them again.

Restore photographs using the details that remain.

We restore damaged areas using details that survive in the photograph and available reference material. People choose which areas to repair so they can preserve the parts of the original they want to keep.

We build all three so people can return to everyday plans, exhibition memories, and the scenes captured in photographs when they need them.
