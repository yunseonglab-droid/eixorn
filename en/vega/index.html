The starting point

When schedules and tasks live across separate apps, every conversation starts by reconstructing context. Vega brings that context together with defined access boundaries.

How it works

  1. Review what is ahead

    Grant access on your Mac to see Apple Calendar events, dated reminders and tasks saved in Vega in the Today view. You can also ask “What’s on my schedule today?” in conversation.

  2. Capture a note while you work

    Ask Vega to save a task such as “Check the QR code placement at the gallery entrance.” Use notes to capture decisions and details you will need later, then return to them in the notes list.

  3. Prepare for the next task

    Check your next event and pull up earlier notes to prepare for it. Before installing an exhibition, for example, you can retrieve the notes you saved about the visitor route and QR placement.

Features

Today view and briefings
Review upcoming events and dated reminders, and get an overview in the morning briefing. Apple Calendar and Reminders connections are read-only on Mac.
Schedules and notes in conversation
Save an event or task in Vega and retrieve the notes you took while working. Vega distinguishes its own events from the Apple Calendar events it reads on Mac.
Text and voice
Ask a quick question in text or use voice when typing is impractical. The voice connection you choose can use an external service.
Access controls for tools
Vega follows the permissions set for tools such as schedules, notes and documents. For supported tools, you can disable access, allow requested actions, or require confirmation before they run.
