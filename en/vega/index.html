Vega

An AI that keeps up with your day.

A personal AI that brings schedules, tasks, notes and conversation together. See what matters today and carry the context forward in a conversation.

Inside VegaApp screens with sample data

The starting point

When schedules and tasks live across separate apps, every conversation starts by reconstructing context. Vega brings that context together with defined access boundaries.

Design and engineering

Today at a glance
See calendar events and tasks together. Apple Calendar events are displayed read-only when permission is granted on Mac.
Notes and voice
Save and retrieve tasks and notes in conversation. Text and voice are supported, and some voice connections can use external services.
Permission boundaries
A local model can be used. Document and source lookups have defined scopes, and personal schedules and notes use dedicated tools.
