Personal AI

Vega

A personal AI that brings schedules, tasks, notes and conversation together. See what matters today and carry the context forward in a conversation.

Read the case study+

The starting point

When schedules and tasks live across separate apps, every conversation starts by reconstructing context. Vega brings that context together with defined access boundaries.

Design and engineering

Today at a glance
See calendar events and tasks together. Apple Calendar events are displayed read-only when permission is granted on Mac.
Notes and voice
Save and retrieve tasks and notes in conversation. Text and voice are supported, and some voice connections can use external services.
Permission boundaries
A local model can be used. Document and source lookups have defined scopes, and personal schedules and notes use dedicated tools.

Exhibition platform

REM404

A platform for artists and operators to build exhibitions and for visitors to discover artworks and image-tracked AR from a QR code. One exhibition connects preparation, the visit and the archive.

Read the case study+

The starting point

When the exhibition website, on-site AR, artwork management and operational checks are separate, changes on the floor are hard to coordinate. REM404 groups artworks and visitor memories by exhibition and gives each role its own view.

Design and engineering

Visitor web and mobile apps
Visitors can open a QR code or exhibition link without signing in, then explore artwork information, image-tracked AR and visitor memories. The visitor journey spans web, iOS and Android.
Artist and administrator workspaces
Artists manage artwork materials in their assigned exhibitions. Administrators manage roles and the state of exhibitions.
REM404 Center
The desktop tool checks operational status and AR readiness. Operational and development checks have their own workspace.
Exhibition archive
Artworks and visitor memories are organized by exhibition. Exhibitions move into an archived state when their run ends.

Image restoration and enhancement engine

Aether

Restore building façades and repair torn photographs. Add color to black-and-white photos, refine detail, and upscale images up to 4×.

Read the case study+

The starting point

Building façades shift in perspective from one photograph to the next, and fragments of torn photographs rarely line up. Aether provides alignment and restoration tools for both and lets you mark the areas you want to protect.

Design and engineering

Up to 4× upscaling
Enlarge a photo up to 4× and adjust edge detail. Choose between the Aether engine and Real-ESRGAN.
Old photo repair
Mark scratches and torn edges for repair, align displaced fragments, and use the face protection brush to preserve surviving facial features.
Black-and-white and sepia colorization
Add color to black-and-white and sepia photographs, then adjust hue and strength for separate regions such as the subject and background.
Building façade restoration
Align archival references with a present-day photograph to restore damaged or obscured areas. Prioritize pixels visible in the reference and preserve the present-day photograph outside the mask.
Masking and alignment
Define the restoration area with manual brushes or automatic segmentation. Align façade references using OpenCV SIFT or manually placed points. Windows RTX also supports depth-conditioned generative restoration.
Finishing and export
Finish a photo with clone stamping, straightening, and cropping, then save it as a PNG. Façade restorations can also export reference evidence and pixel verification records.

Zero changes outside the mask

We created a test input by obscuring windows and stonework in a public-domain photograph, then used the original as the reference. All 181,845 pixels outside the mask matched the input after a four-point alignment and restoration.
