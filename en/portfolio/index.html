Personal AI

Vega

A personal AI that brings schedules, tasks, notes and conversation together. See what matters today and carry the context forward in a conversation.

Read the case study+

The starting point

When schedules and tasks live across separate apps, every conversation starts by reconstructing context. Vega brings that context together with defined access boundaries.

Design and engineering

Today view and briefings
Review upcoming events and dated reminders, and get an overview in the morning briefing. Apple Calendar and Reminders connections are read-only on Mac.
Schedules and notes in conversation
Save an event or task in Vega and retrieve the notes you took while working. Vega distinguishes its own events from the Apple Calendar events it reads on Mac.
Text and voice
Ask a quick question in text or use voice when typing is impractical. The voice connection you choose can use an external service.
Access controls for tools
Vega follows the permissions set for tools such as schedules, notes and documents. For supported tools, you can disable access, allow requested actions, or require confirmation before they run.

Exhibition platform

REM404

A platform for artists and operators to build exhibitions and for visitors to discover artworks and image-tracked AR from a QR code. One exhibition connects preparation, the visit and the archive.

Read the case study+

The starting point

When the exhibition website, on-site AR, artwork management and operational checks are separate, changes on the floor are hard to coordinate. REM404 groups artworks and visitor memories by exhibition and gives each role its own view.

Design and engineering

Visitor web without an app install
Open artwork and exhibition information from a public link or QR without signing in. AR requires camera access and a registered target image. The organizer’s exhibition settings determine which visitor memory features are available.
Artwork registration and shared management
Artists manage artwork images, descriptions and linked materials for assigned exhibitions. Organizers can invite co-managers and set access, visibility and opening hours.
Visitor memory moderation
Organizers can review memories and reports by exhibition. The management app allows them to hide or remove records and block authors when necessary.
REM404 Center
Use the desktop workspace to check on-site readiness, including access to the visitor view and AR preparation, then review the diagnostics.
Exhibition archive and backups
Manage artwork and visitor records by exhibition and archive shows after closing. Authorized organizers can view saved records and use the supported backup and recovery features.

Image restoration and enhancement engine

Aether

An image engine that repairs damaged photographs and upscales them up to 4×.

Read the case study+
Design and engineering

The starting point

Building façades shift in perspective from one photograph to the next, and fragments of torn photographs rarely line up. Aether provides alignment and restoration tools for both and lets you mark the areas you want to protect.

Up to 4× upscaling
Increase the width and height of a photograph by up to 4×. Set the scale and edge detail separately, then inspect the result at a larger size to balance texture and sharpness.
Old photo repair
Align displaced fragments and mark the tear. Aether fills the gap using the direction and texture of surviving pixels. The face protection brush shields surviving eyes, nose and mouth during tear repair and detail enhancement.
Black-and-white and sepia colorization
Correct sepia tones and estimate skin color using the original brightness. Mark skin, clothing and background independently to adjust color and strength, or sample a color from a reference photograph.
Building façade restoration
Align archival references with a present-day photograph to restore damaged or obscured areas. Prioritize pixels visible in the reference and preserve the present-day photograph outside the mask.
Masking and alignment
Draw a mask by hand or mark points inside and outside the building to generate a segmentation candidate. Align façades using OpenCV SIFT features or manual points, and keep the present-day image where the reference offers insufficient evidence.
Finishing and export
Retouch small marks with the healing brush and clone stamp, then adjust the crop and level. For a façade restoration, refine brightness and saturation, save a separate version, and compare it with the original or a previous result.

Zero changes outside the mask

We created a test input by obscuring windows and stonework in a public-domain photograph, then used the original as the reference. All 181,845 pixels outside the mask matched the input after a four-point alignment and restoration.
