Image restoration and enhancement engine

Aether

Restore photos and bring out the detail.

Restore building façades and repair torn photographs. Add color to black-and-white photos, refine detail, and upscale images up to 4×.

The starting point

Building façades shift in perspective from one photograph to the next, and fragments of torn photographs rarely line up. Aether provides alignment and restoration tools for both and lets you mark the areas you want to protect.

Design and engineering

Up to 4× upscaling
Enlarge a photo up to 4× and adjust edge detail. Choose between the Aether engine and Real-ESRGAN.
Old photo repair
Mark scratches and torn edges for repair, align displaced fragments, and use the face protection brush to preserve surviving facial features.
Black-and-white and sepia colorization
Add color to black-and-white and sepia photographs, then adjust hue and strength for separate regions such as the subject and background.
Building façade restoration
Align archival references with a present-day photograph to restore damaged or obscured areas. Prioritize pixels visible in the reference and preserve the present-day photograph outside the mask.
Masking and alignment
Define the restoration area with manual brushes or automatic segmentation. Align façade references using OpenCV SIFT or manually placed points. Windows RTX also supports depth-conditioned generative restoration.
Finishing and export
Finish a photo with clone stamping, straightening, and cropping, then save it as a PNG. Façade restorations can also export reference evidence and pixel verification records.

Experience and verification

Zero changes outside the mask

We created a test input by obscuring windows and stonework in a public-domain photograph, then used the original as the reference. All 181,845 pixels outside the mask matched the input after a four-point alignment and restoration.
