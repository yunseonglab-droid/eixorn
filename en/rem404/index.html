REM404

Exhibition platform

A digital experience that starts in the gallery.

A platform for artists and operators to build exhibitions and for visitors to discover artworks and image-tracked AR from a QR code. One exhibition connects preparation, the visit and the archive.

Visit REM404.com

The original REM404 project

Memories and Traces of Those Who Remained

REM404 began with Yunseong Um’s photographs of spaces people had left behind. The project overlays a reimagined scene on a photograph of a closed school and gives each viewer a place to add a memory of their own.

  1. Register the work

    The original project paired a photograph of an abandoned building with a restored image to reveal after recognition. That pairing informed how today’s management app separates the target photograph from the image or video the audience sees.

  2. Enter from a QR code

    Visitors open the mobile site from a QR code and point their phone at the photograph. When the work is recognized, the linked scene appears over it.

  3. Leave a memory

    Visitors can leave a short anonymous memory inspired by the photograph. The last part of the experience lets them read someone else’s memory or move on to another work.

What Yunseong Um designed

At a photography exhibition, Um noticed how briefly visitors paused before moving to the next image. He wanted the stories that surfaced in front of a photograph to remain available after the visit. He designed how an artist’s photograph becomes an AR target, how a QR code opens the experience without an app install, and how visitors leave and read anonymous memories after viewing.

The starting point

When the exhibition website, on-site AR, artwork management and operational checks are separate, changes on the floor are hard to coordinate. REM404 groups artworks and visitor memories by exhibition and gives each role its own view.

How it works

  1. Set up the exhibition and its works

    Set the dates, location and introduction in the management app, then add the photograph and text for each artwork. For AR works, link the image that the camera should recognize and the image or video that visitors should see after recognition.

  2. Visitors enter from a QR code

    Once the exhibition is public, visitors open the venue’s QR code or a shared link. They can read about the works on the mobile web and use the camera to experience linked scenes where AR is available.

  3. Keep the visit with the exhibition

    When memory submissions are enabled, visitors can leave a thought or personal memory by an artwork. Organizers manage the artwork materials and visitor records by exhibition, and archive the exhibition once it closes.

Features

Visitor web without an app install
Open artwork and exhibition information from a public link or QR without signing in. AR requires camera access and a registered target image. The organizer’s exhibition settings determine which visitor memory features are available.
Artwork registration and shared management
Artists manage artwork images, descriptions and linked materials for assigned exhibitions. Organizers can invite co-managers and set access, visibility and opening hours.
Visitor memory moderation
Organizers can review memories and reports by exhibition. The management app allows them to hide or remove records and block authors when necessary.
REM404 Center
Use the desktop workspace to check on-site readiness, including access to the visitor view and AR preparation, then review the diagnostics.
Exhibition archive and backups
Manage artwork and visitor records by exhibition and archive shows after closing. Authorized organizers can view saved records and use the supported backup and recovery features.
